Diffusion MRI Denoising and Reconstruction

  • I developed a directionally aligned k-space filter for 30-direction DTI, increasing SNR by 39.89% at tested input SNR 55 and reducing FA-map RMSE/MAE degradation rates by 46.3%/47.6% in simulations.
  • I compared polar Fourier and Cartesian regridding reconstructions, diagnosed implementation artifacts, and acquired phantom data on a Siemens Trio MRI system.
  • I first-authored the resulting ISMRM 2024 abstract and ICEE 2024 IEEE paper, drafted both works, recorded the ISMRM presentation, and presented at ICEE.
